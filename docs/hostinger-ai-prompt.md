# Prompts for Hostinger's AI website builder

Two versions. Hostinger's first "describe your website" box is short, so use
**Prompt A** to generate, then paste **Prompt B** into the AI editor to refine.

Everything here is taken from the built app, not invented. Keep it that way —
the previous site described a product that did not exist.

---

## Prompt A — the short one (generation step)

> Ease is a menstrual health app for women managing PCOS. It syncs cycle dates
> with nutrition, exercise, sleep and stress guidance, so users stop guessing why
> their body behaves the way it does. Launching soon on iOS and Android in India.
> Build a calm, premium pre-launch site that collects waitlist signups.
>
> Audience: women 18–40 with irregular cycles, acne, hair fall, fatigue and
> bloating, who find health advice overwhelming and contradictory.
>
> Tone: warm, direct, grown-up. Never girly, never clinical, never hype.
>
> Pages: Home, How it works, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use.

## Prompt B — the full brief (refinement step)

### Brand

Name: **Ease**. Domain: easewellness.org. Tagline direction: *"Your cycle, your
food, your energy — finally in one place."*

Positioning: bright, bold and beautiful in a way that feels natural and elite.
**Not** pastel, not flowery, not "self-care" clichés. No pink. No illustrations
of flowers or moons. Think a serious health product that happens to be beautiful.

### Who it is for

A founder or creator in her late twenties or thirties. Irregular periods,
hair fall, acne, inflammation. She has read a hundred contradictory things
about PCOS and has no appetite for another one. She wants to know what to do
today, not a syllabus. Lifestyle change currently feels like a second job on top
of a demanding first one.

Speak to her as a capable adult who is tired, not as a patient or a teenager.

### Colours and type

- Tangerine `#F2701C` — primary, used confidently for headlines and buttons
- Deep blue `#4E6E8E` — secondary accent only
- Bone `#FFFDFB` — page background, roughly 70% of the surface
- Ink `#14110F` — body text
- Headlines: **Bodoni Moda** (high-contrast serif)
- Body: **Jost** (geometric sans)

Generous white space. One idea per section. No gradients, no drop shadows on
text, no stock photography of women laughing with salad.

### What the app actually does

**Free**
- Daily check-in: bleeding, symptoms and severity, energy, mood, sleep, stress, movement, notes
- A cycle ring showing the current day and phase
- Calendar with cycle phases, logged and predicted period days, and Google Calendar events alongside them
- Nutrition guidance tied to the current phase
- Basic cycle length and regularity charts

**Premium — ₹699/month or ₹6,999/year, seven-day free trial**
- The full body protocol: exercise, stress, metabolism, skin and hair
- Detailed pattern charts across every symptom tracked
- Correlations: what is genuinely moving the symptoms, tested statistically on the device
- Trophies and badges for logging streaks
- Own events and tasks planned around the cycle

### The three things that make it different — lead with these

1. **It does not pretend to know things it doesn't.** With few logged cycles it
   says "around day 17", not "day 17". It never shows invented data before the
   first period is logged.
2. **It tells her when a cycle is running long** instead of silently restarting
   the count — the signal that matters most with PCOS, and the one most apps hide.
3. **Patterns are tested, not asserted.** It checks 21 relationships across
   sleep, stress, nutrition, movement and every symptom, and reports only what
   holds up — including saying plainly when nothing does.

### Page structure

**Home** — hero with tagline and waitlist form; the three differentiators above;
a short "what you get free vs premium" comparison; closing waitlist call.

**How it works** — the daily check-in, the cycle ring, the calendar, the patterns.
Four sections, one screenshot each.

**Privacy Policy** — slug must be exactly `privacy`
**Terms of Use** — slug must be exactly `terms`

Both legal pages: paste the supplied text verbatim. Do not rewrite, summarise or
"improve" it. Hide both from the main navigation; link them in the footer only.

**Footer** — Privacy, Terms, support email `easetrackerapp@gmail.com`,
Instagram, LinkedIn.

### Hard rules — do not break these

- **Invent nothing.** No testimonials, no reviews, no names, no user counts, no
  ratings, no "trusted by" logos, no press mentions. The app has no users yet.
- **No App Store or Google Play badges, and no "available now".** It has not
  launched. Say "launching soon" and collect emails.
- **No medical claims.** Ease does not treat, cure, manage, reverse or diagnose
  PCOS or anything else. Do not write "balance your hormones", "fix your cycle"
  or "heal your gut". It tracks, shows patterns, and offers general guidance.
- **Never present it as contraception or a fertility tool.** It does not detect
  ovulation and must not be described as helping anyone conceive or avoid
  pregnancy.
- No countdown timers, no "limited spots", no urgency tricks.
- India only for now. Prices in ₹.
